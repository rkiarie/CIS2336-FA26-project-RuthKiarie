Welcome to the UH Sugar Land Student Center

A new home on campus to study, meet up, recharge and get involved, opening for Cougars in Fort Bend County.

Book a Study Room

What's Inside

The center is built around what Sugar Land students asked for: quiet places to study, open spaces to connect, and help when they need it.

Bookable Rooms

Five rooms, from two-person study pods to a 16-seat conference room, with screens and whiteboards.

Cougar Lounge

Open seating, charging stations and campus Wi-Fi for the time between classes.

Red & White Cafe

Coffee, grab-and-go meals and snacks, with student pricing.

Events & Orgs

Space for student organizations, career fairs, workshops and game nights.

Take a Quick Look

Watch a short preview of the kind of open, light-filled space we're building for the student center.

The center sits at the heart of the Sugar Land campus, a short walk from the academic buildings and parking.
