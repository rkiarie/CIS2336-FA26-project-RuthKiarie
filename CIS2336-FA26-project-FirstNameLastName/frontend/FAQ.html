Any currently enrolled UH student can use the center. Faculty and staff are welcome too. Guests must be with a student and sign in at the front desk.

Use the Room Booking page. Choose one of the five rooms, a date and a time, and enter your UH email and PSID. You'll see a confirmation or a decline right away.

Each booking can last up to 3 hours, between 8:00 AM and 9:00 PM. You can book up to 30 days ahead. Rooms not claimed within 15 minutes of the start time are released.

Registered student organizations can book the Conference Room for meetings of up to 16 people. For larger events, contact the center staff at least two weeks ahead to reserve the lounge.
